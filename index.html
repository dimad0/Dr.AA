Areas of focus

Care for the conditions that are hardest to live with

Every plan starts with a careful diagnosis. From there, treatment is matched to your history, your goals, and what has — or hasn’t — worked before.

Treatment-resistant depression

When depression hasn’t lifted after medications or therapy, a fresh, thorough review can open new options — including TMS and other neuromodulation.

Anxiety disorders

Generalized anxiety, panic, and social anxiety can quietly shape every part of life. Treatment aims for relief you can feel day to day.

Obsessive-compulsive disorder

Evidence-based care for intrusive thoughts and compulsions, combining medication management with specialized therapy when it helps.

Bipolar & mood disorders

Steady, long-term care to stabilize mood, reduce relapses, and find the right balance between effectiveness and side effects.

Addiction & substance use

Nonjudgmental, medically informed treatment for substance use — and for the anxiety, depression, or trauma that so often comes with it.

Serious mental illness

Experienced, respectful care for schizophrenia, schizoaffective disorder, and other serious psychiatric conditions, coordinated with your wider care team.

The approach

Patient-centered, never one-size-fits-all

Dr. Awerbuch pairs a careful, unhurried assessment with a personalized plan — and, where appropriate, integrates newer treatments for people who haven’t found relief.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Your first visit is a comprehensive psychiatric evaluation — your history, symptoms, past treatments, physical health, and what you want to change.

  2. 02

    Personalize

    Together you build a plan that fits your life: thoughtful medication management, coordination with your therapist or primary care physician, and clear next steps.

  3. 03

    Advance

    When standard treatments haven’t been enough, care can include advanced options such as Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) and other neuromodulation techniques.

Meet your psychiatrist

Adam W. Awerbuch, MD, MBA

Dr. Awerbuch earned a dual MD/MBA at the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine and completed his psychiatry residency at the University of Miami and Jackson Memorial Hospital.

During training he cared for patients with serious mental illness, substance use disorders, and HIV, and researched ways to better identify and support survivors of human trafficking. Today he divides his time between outpatient practice at TheraMind Center of Santa Barbara and the Department of Psychiatry at Santa Barbara Cottage Hospital.

  • Adult psychiatry with a focus on difficult-to-treat depression, anxiety, and OCD
  • Particular interest in addiction medicine and serious mental illness
  • Experience with TMS and other neuromodulation techniques

When depression hasn’t responded

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS)

TMS is a non-invasive, FDA-cleared treatment for major depression. It uses focused magnetic pulses to stimulate areas of the brain involved in mood regulation.

  • Non-invasiveNo anesthesia, no sedation — you’re awake and comfortable throughout.
  • In-officeSessions take place at TheraMind Center, and you can drive yourself home after.
  • Drug-free optionCan be used alongside or instead of medication, depending on your plan.
