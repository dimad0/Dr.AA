About Dr. Awerbuch

Adam W. Awerbuch, MD, MBA

A Santa Barbara psychiatrist trained at one of the largest public teaching hospitals in the United States — with a commitment to patients who haven’t yet found the help they need.

Biography

Dr. Adam Awerbuch is a psychiatrist practicing in Santa Barbara, California. His work in adult psychiatry focuses on treatment-resistant depression, anxiety disorders, obsessive-compulsive disorder, and other mood disorders, with a particular interest in addiction medicine and the care of people living with serious mental illness.

He attended the University of Miami Leonard M. Miller School of Medicine, where he earned a dual MD/MBA degree, concentrating his business studies in healthcare administration. He went on to complete his psychiatry residency at the University of Miami and Jackson Memorial Hospital in Miami, Florida.

Residency placed him on the front lines of psychiatric care. He worked closely with patients facing substance use disorders and with people living with HIV, treated patients with serious mental illness, and researched interventions to assist victims of human trafficking — work that contributed to peer-reviewed publications on how clinicians can better recognize and care for survivors.

In 2024, Dr. Awerbuch came to Santa Barbara, joining the medical staff of the Department of Psychiatry at Santa Barbara Cottage Hospital, where he cares for patients in the inpatient setting. That September he also began seeing patients at TheraMind Center of Santa Barbara, a practice dedicated to advanced treatment of depression and other mood disorders. There, his experience with Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) and other neuromodulation techniques allows him to offer options beyond medication alone.

Philosophy of care

Dr. Awerbuch is committed to a patient-centered approach to mental health. That means taking the time to understand the whole person, building a personalized care plan rather than following a template, and — where appropriate — integrating cutting-edge treatments for people who haven’t responded to standard care.

His business training adds a practical understanding of how healthcare systems work, which helps when coordinating care among therapists, primary care physicians, hospitals, and insurers.

Research & publications

Advocacy through research

During his training, Dr. Awerbuch contributed to research on how healthcare and other professionals can better recognize and care for survivors of human trafficking.

  • Trauma, Violence, & Abuse · 2020

    Informing Human Trafficking Clinical Care Through Two Systematic Reviews on Sexual Assault and Intimate Partner Violence

    Menon B, Stoklosa H, Van Dommelen K, Awerbuch A, Caddell L, Roberts K, Potter J. Addresses the lack of evidence on the clinical management of patients who have experienced human trafficking.

  • International Journal of Human Rights in Healthcare · 2020

    Raising Awareness of Human Trafficking in Key Professional Fields via a Multidisciplinary Educational Approach

    Vol. 13, No. 2, pp. 159–169. Evaluated a one-day educational conference in Miami for medical, law enforcement, social work, and policy professionals, finding a multidisciplinary approach may be effective in raising awareness of human trafficking.
