## Design specificity verdict

**Largely interchangeable.** Swap the name and the condition list and this could be any solo therapy or wellness practice. It is a competent version of the category default, not a design that comes from this practice or this visitor.

**Design review.**
- One recipe repeats everywhere: a small uppercase label, then a serif heading with an italic teal ending, then an intro paragraph (assets/css/styles.css:627-645, :160-165). All six homepage headings use the italic ending.
- The homepage is the standard landing-page sequence: split hero (index.html:103-143), credentials strip (:146-167), three-column icon cards (:178-209), dark numbered band (:221-246), photo-left bio (:249-273), feature panel (:276-301), call-to-action band (:304-315).
- The business card's identity is barely used. Aqua, the Futura-style wordmark and the knot logo appear only in the 37px header mark and the button colour. Sand, sage, arches and serif italics do the rest.

- The arch motif frames the hero, the portrait, every icon tile, every page hero and the CTA band.
- What is specific: the crisis bar, the FAQ questions, the same-building detail and the Good Faith Estimate notice.

## Overall impression

The site is calm, careful and honest, and its best page (new patients) is written for exactly the chosen visitor. But the homepage talks to someone else, and the main button on every page leads to a form that turns people away. The biggest opportunity is to make "what happens when you reach out" the centre of the homepage and make sure reaching out works.

## What's working

- The new-patients page asks the visitor's real questions in their words (new-patients.html:177-217): psychiatrist versus therapist, referrals, "will I have to take medication?", confidentiality. "Bring what you can" (:147) lowers the stakes.
- The safety and accessibility groundwork is sound. The crisis bar is persistent but quiet, the menu closes with Escape, reduced motion is honoured, nothing overflows at 320px, and form validation never wipes what was typed.
- Restraint in claims. No testimonials, statistics, stock people or board-certification claim. Lines like "That isn't a personal failure" (services.html:116) are the right voice.

## Priority issues

### [P0] The main button on every page leads to a form that cannot send

- **What:** seven "Request an appointment" links point to `contact.html#request`. The form has `data-endpoint=""` (contact.html:155). After full validation it shows "Thank you. Online requests are not yet being received… please call" (assets/js/main.js:153-161). It also shows ten controls at once, requires phone even when Email is the chosen contact method (contact.html:169, :182), asks for a diagnosis category (:200-209), and gives no word on who replies or when.
- **Why it matters:** for someone who finds reaching out hard, this is a rejection at the moment of commitment. Many won't try again.
- **Fix:** until a HIPAA-compliant destination exists, replace the form with a "Reach the office" block (call or email) that says what happens next, and relabel the buttons. When the form returns, cut it to name, one contact method and an optional note. Never open a failure with "Thank you."
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden contact.html`

### [P1] The homepage speaks to people who have already failed treatment

- **What:** "treatment-resistant" is in the hero paragraph (index.html:109) and the first card (:181). TMS or neuromodulation appears in the credentials bar (:163), approach step 3 (:242), the bio checklist (:267) and a dedicated panel (:276-301). Headings say "hardest to live with" (:174) and "complex conditions" (services.html:75). First-timers are acknowledged once, in the last section (index.html:308). The homepage body never links to new-patients.html.
- **Why it matters:** a first-timer with ordinary anxiety concludes the practice is for more severe cases. It also picks a positioning the owner has not chosen.
- **Fix:** put the four "getting started" steps (new-patients.html:115-136) directly under the hero. Change the second hero button from "Meet Dr. Awerbuch" to "What to expect". Reduce conditions to a plain list that includes "not sure". Move TMS and credentials to their own pages.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable shape index.html`

### [P1] Phone, email and form are not equals, and on a phone the office number is hidden

- **What:** at 960px and below the header call button moves into the menu (assets/css/styles.css:418-477), so the only tappable numbers on the first mobile screen are 988 and 911. The first inline tap-to-call on the mobile homepage is in the final CTA band. Email appears on the homepage only in the footer (index.html:349). On the contact page the form gets a prominent card while phone and email are two of five rows beside fax and hours (contact.html:87-128).
- **Why it matters:** all three channels are meant to count equally. The design chooses the form for the visitor, and the form is the one that doesn't work.
- **Fix:** one three-option block (Call, Email, Send a request), each with a line on what happens next, used in the hero, the closing band and the top of the contact page. Keep a call button visible in the mobile header.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

### [P2] Accessibility gaps that contradict what the site claims

- **What:**
  - The focus ring (#08739a) measures about 2.5:1 on the crisis bar and footer (assets/css/styles.css:124-128), including the 988 and 911 links.
  - Form error messages are not linked to their fields: no id, no `aria-describedby` (contact.html:160-175, :196, :233; assets/js/main.js:109-116).
  - Text inputs remove the outline and substitute a faint border change (assets/css/styles.css:1821-1828).
  - The open mobile menu does not contain focus (assets/js/main.js:18-25).
  - Insurance placeholder contrast is 4.35:1.
- **Why it matters:** the README and PRODUCT.md both say form errors are announced to assistive technology. They aren't.
- **Fix:** use a light focus ring on dark surfaces, link each error to its field, restore the ring on inputs, and contain focus in the open menu. Correct the PRODUCT.md line.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

### [P2] Template look, with a placeholder shown as content

- **What:** the repeated label-heading-italic recipe, arches on nearly everything, and a grey silhouette up to 440px wide under "Meet your psychiatrist" (index.html:256, about.html:88). Twelve cards lift on hover but are not links (assets/css/styles.css:965).
- **Why it matters:** trust in a solo practice rests on a person. A blank avatar reads as unfinished.
- **Fix:** remove the portrait slot until a real headshot exists and make the bio text-led. Vary section structure. Let the card's aqua, wordmark and knot carry more of the identity. Make cards links or drop the hover lift.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`
