Urgent help

If you need help right now

The office cannot respond to emergencies. If you or someone you know is in danger, please use one of these resources immediately.

988 Lifeline

Call or text 988, or chat at 988lifeline.org (opens in a new tab). Free, confidential support 24/7.

Emergency

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room, such as Santa Barbara Cottage Hospital.

Santa Barbara County

Behavioral Wellness 24/7 Crisis & Access Line: (888) 868-1649.
